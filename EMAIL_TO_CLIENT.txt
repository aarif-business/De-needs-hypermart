WHAT WE BUILT FOR YOU

1. HOMEPAGE
   A high-impact landing page with your store's branding (yellow & red), a hero section

2. PRODUCT CATALOGUE
   All your products are listed with images, prices, discount badges, and category filters.

   Snacks & Beverages, Home & Kitchen, Personal Care, and Home Decor.

   A sticky search bar at the top lets customers instantly find any product.

   - Update order status (Pending → Confirmed → Delivered)
   - Monitor low-stock alerts on the dashboard
